# Creative QA

Distinguish structural correctness, audiovisual quality, complete playability and real-device validation, with evidence for every conclusion.

## Different checks answer different questions

Build checks show that the program can produce output; structural tests check states and protocols; motion review checks performance and shot transitions; a full walkthrough tests whether the interaction works.

## Attach scope and evidence to every conclusion

Use four statuses: passed, failed, unverified and not applicable. Record the version, subject, method and evidence. Desktop simulation is not the same as real-device testing.

## Preserve approved behavior after revisions

Retest the failed transition, its neighboring connections and related branches. Tie each issue to a specific object rather than a vague request to “improve everything.”
