# Screen Direction

Translate an established script into visual design, assets, storyboards, blocking, sound and model prompts, giving every shot a narrative purpose.

## Carry forward approved story and assets

Read the current script, characters, references and handoff. Respect established causality, essential dialogue and reveal order, explicitly flagging any proposed story changes.

## Make shot language executable

Plan framing, position, movement, gaze, action and sound around the information or emotion each shot conveys. Establish visual continuity and character motivation separately, then bring them together in the scene.

## Deliver within actual model capabilities and scope

Write prompts and reference roles for the selected model version. Check duration, sound, asset dependencies and delivery format; distinguish plans, generated outputs and accepted footage.
