# Vicky Drama

Connect character, performance, camera and branching to turn viewing into a responsive interactive story.

## Let choices happen through performance

Establish why the user makes a choice at this moment, then design the character’s visible response. Branches should change behavior and lasting facts, not simply swap buttons and dialogue.

## Keep characters, space and sound continuous

Preserve approved identities, clothing, props, positions, eyelines and sound sources. In pure POV projects, change framing through natural gaze, distance and movement.

## Review the experience as a continuous whole

Scripts, structural checks and extracted frames each have a purpose, but cannot replace watching in motion. Rejoin revisions to the complete series and check sound, performance and transitions after choices.
