# Narrative Momentum

Connect character desire, initiative and the consequences of choices, reducing mechanical compliance and repetitive tension so the story keeps evolving.

## Turn character adjectives into actions

Define what the character wants now, why they act, their preferred strategy and what would change it. Preserve their own motives and knowledge limits while responding to the user.

## Create a new situation through consequences

Give events prerequisites, motives, actions, intervention points and completion conditions. Carry forward prior consequences and pay off expectations instead of resetting relationships through forgetfulness or repeated misunderstandings.

## Test initiative and meaningful choice differences

Check whether the story advances with minimal input and produces different outcomes when the user makes a meaningful choice. Distinguish text simulation, generated video, playback review and user testing.
