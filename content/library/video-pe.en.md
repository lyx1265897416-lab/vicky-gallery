# Prompt as Direction

Turn “natural, intimate and cinematic” into shot instructions a model can execute and a creator can review.

## Give each reference a clear purpose

Bind references separately to identity, wardrobe, space, starting state, motion and sound. Avoid importing unrelated characters or expressions from reference images.

## Make behavior specific and camera movement motivated

Describe who perceives what and how they respond, including hands, eyelines, props and distance. Specify camera path, viewing direction, focus, motivation and stopping position.

## Define checkable entry and exit states

Record the actual starting state and planned exit. The next shot continues from the reviewed output, not from an outcome that has yet to happen.

## Template

```text
References: identity / wardrobe / location / actual start state.
Start: positions, gaze, hands, props and current activity.
Trigger: the exact user-selected meaning.
Performance: visible reception, response, action and aftermath.
Speech: exact dialogue, speaker, delivery and pauses.
Camera: position/path, target, focus, start and endpoint.
End: hands, distance, objects and continuing activity.
Protect: this shot’s specific continuity constraints.
```
