# Story Openings

Translate emotional needs, relationships and conflict from drama research into an interactive opening: who the user is, why it matters and what they can do now.

## Turn the desire to watch into a reason to participate

Identify emotional needs and relationship tension in the references, then define the user’s place in the story. Interaction must produce a visible response beyond adding buttons to a clip.

## Check the opening with five questions

Who am I, where am I, what can I do, why do I care, and why is it my turn now? Use shot and dialogue evidence to distinguish unclear communication from a missing motivation.

## Hand the next action to the user

Establish the immediate situation, then pause where the user can respond. Hand off user and character knowledge, current state and available actions; check cooperative, questioning and free-form first responses.
