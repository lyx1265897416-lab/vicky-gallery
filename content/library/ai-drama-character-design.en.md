# Character Design

Use audience and genre evidence to design appearance, ensemble distinctions and identity references, then assess stability across performance and consecutive shots.

## Define the character’s visual purpose

Define the identity, temperament and dramatic tension the first impression should convey. Separate audience evidence, creative hypotheses and personal taste before comparing distinct design directions.

## Build distinct characters and clear references

Differentiate silhouettes, facial features, hair, posture, garment structure and materials. Give identity, wardrobe and style references distinct roles to avoid conflicting visual guidance.

## Validate the design in shots

Check recognition in profile, expressions, speech, ordinary indoor light and story actions. Record versions and selection reasons without presenting design hypotheses as validated audience findings.
