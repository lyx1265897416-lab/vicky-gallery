# Acting Direction

Shape micro-expressions, action and voice around character goals and partner cues, connecting adjacent shots through an observable performance.

## Start with what the character wants from their partner

Define each shot’s purpose, objective, obstacle and strategy. Shift the beat when new information arrives or a strategy fails; include the listener’s reception and reaction in the performance.

## Write emotion as a visible process

Move from stimulus and reception to a readable reaction, active response and aftermath using a few precise actions. Match gaze, props, blocking, light and sound to the actual previous endpoint.

## Choose revisions from evidence

Separate observed problems from possible causes and assess continuity, accuracy and plausibility. Choose pickups, regeneration or other revisions within project constraints, then recheck protected content, seams and adjacent shots.
