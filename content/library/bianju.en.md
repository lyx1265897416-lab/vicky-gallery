# Story Development

Connect ideas, worldbuilding, character motives, episode structure and dialogue in one workflow, selecting relevant professional references and preserving handoffs.

## Begin with the story promise and conflict

Establish genre, audience and core conflict. Let world rules, character desires and the cost of choices generate the story, preserving established decisions.

## Let actions cause consequences

Build episodes around actions, consequences and new information that change decisions. Pay off hooks; write character-specific dialogue and express emotion through playable behavior.

## Reconnect revisions to one shared story

Review motives, causality, established facts, setups and pacing within the revision scope. Preserve decisions, open issues and next steps, distinguishing reference material from new creative work.
